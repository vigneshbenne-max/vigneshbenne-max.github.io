About Me

Bridging Foundations with Real-World Code

A student's perspective on exploring artificial intelligence through curiosity, experimentation, and honest effort.

Hello! I am Benne Vignesh, a second-year undergraduate pursuing my B.Tech degree with an active focus on Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning.

My journey began with curious questions about how modern computational systems understand language and numerical trends. Rather than merely consuming high-level theory, I strongly believe that the most solid understanding comes from building practical projects—writing code, preprocessing messy data, tuning algorithms, and seeing where models succeed or fail.

I take pride in writing clear, understandable Python code, exploring core machine learning libraries, and documenting my learning process honestly. Outside of structured coursework, I spend my time experimenting with local machine learning prototypes and exploring new ways to solve genuine everyday challenges with software.

Applied Machine Learning

Constructing practical models using algorithms like Naive Bayes and vectorizers like TF-IDF, connecting math to working code.

Data Understanding

Investigating structured numerical and textual data using pandas and NumPy to uncover distributions and anomalies.

End-to-End Prototypes

Serving trained models through lightweight web interfaces such as Flask and Streamlit to make ML interactive.
